PRIVACY POLICY

YOUR PRIVACY.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

AFTR lets people create and join shared photo collections called “Dumps.” This Privacy Policy explains what information AFTR collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide while using AFTR, including your display name, photos you choose to upload, Dump names, invite codes, and the information needed to associate your device with a Dump. We also process technical information required to operate the app, such as a device identifier and basic service logs.

3. Camera and photo library

AFTR requests access to your camera only when you choose to scan a QR code, and requests access to your photo library only when you choose photos to add to a Dump. AFTR does not access your camera or library unless you grant permission.

4. How we use information

We use information to operate AFTR, create and display Dumps, let people join via invitations, store and deliver uploaded photos, maintain the security and reliability of the service, and respond to support requests.

5. Sharing

Photos, display names, and other content you add to a Dump are visible to people who have access to that Dump. We use service providers to host and store application data. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide AFTR and meet legal or operational requirements. You may delete photos you have uploaded through the app. To request deletion of other personal information, contact us using the address below.

7. Children

AFTR is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as AFTR changes. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hugo@joinaftr.app.
