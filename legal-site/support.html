SUPPORT

Need help with AFTR, a Dump, or your account on this device? We’re here.

CONTACT

Email support@joinaftr.app

We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.

REPORT CONTENT OR A USER

In the app: open a photo → Report, or open Members → Block. You can also email us with the Dump name, approximate time, and what happened.

COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I join a Dump?
Scan the host’s QR code, or open an invite link and install / open AFTR.

I can’t see a Dump anymore
The host may have closed it or removed you. Ask the host for a new invite.

Delete my data
Email support@joinaftr.app from the address you used if applicable, or describe the device and Dumps involved. See our Privacy Policy.

SAFETY

  • Don’t share illegal or non-consensual imagery.
  • Hosts can remove members and photos from a Dump.
  • You can block someone so their content is hidden for you.
