Photo dumps for parties and nights out

What it is

AFTR is where a night’s photos live together — from everyone who was there — without the noise of a public feed. Create a Dump, invite the room, and collect every angle.

How it works

The host creates a Dump and shares a QR code or link. Guests join, add their photos, and browse the shared dump. Simple, fast, built for the night.
